# Vexon IT Services — residential and business scope

Current repository policy · October 19, 2026

This reference follows the owner-approved residential offer recorded in the
October 15 business playbook and the October 19 direction to keep home and
business prices separate. It is not a signed contract, booking, proof of
operational readiness or confirmation that the production site has been updated.

## Residential: personal home use

**Remote PC support: $89 per session, one PC, one main issue, up to 60 minutes.**

- For a home user's personal PC. Work for a business, employer, organization or
  business account is reviewed as business work, even from a home office.
- Confirm fit, ownership/authority, consent, commercial tool/license suitability,
  availability, appointment, scope and written total before booking.
- Covers agreed time and assessment, not a guaranteed repair. Unused time does
  not expand the job to more devices or issues.
- Existing Wi-Fi, printer/device help, home visits and other setup are **by
  quote**. Travel, materials and extra work are not included in the remote offer.
- Migrations, data recovery, network redesign, business accounts and specialist
  work require a separate fit review and quote. No recurring home plan is offered.
- Before booking, confirm applicable tax and separate costs in the written total,
  plus payment and cancellation/refund terms. Additional work needs prior approval.

## Business: separate fit review and quote

The residential price is **not** a business callout, project, per-user,
per-device or monthly rate. It is not a credit toward or enrollment in a plan.

Business projects are separately scoped and quoted after fit review. Managed
IT pilots and co-managed help are **being prepared**, not active coverage.
There is no approved recurring business price in this repository. No included
onsite allowance, unlimited help, staffed 24/7 service, emergency response,
guaranteed response/fix/recovery time or compliance certification is established.

Before accepting a managed pilot, agree in writing on:

- Customer, authorized approver, listed devices, users, sites and duties.
- Tasks, exclusions, maintenance cadence, support allowance and service hours.
- Actual capacity, escalation owner, change approval and acceptance tests.
- Licensed tools, software, storage, onsite work, travel, materials, onboarding,
  existing issues, applicable tax and the complete price.
- Payment, term, renewal, cancellation/refunds, data handling, customer-owned
  accounts, access removal, records handoff and exit.

Validate commercial tool rights, access consent/revocation, ticket routing,
billing and any promised backup/restore process on authorized test data first.
An alert is not a staffed response. A backup job is not proof of recovery.

## Planning calls and authorization

A free **20-minute planning call** is a needs discussion only, not troubleshooting,
a technical assessment, remote access, changes or a service appointment.
No request alone reserves time, authorizes access, enrolls a plan or authorizes
payment. Record the approved scope, tool, access period and consent before access.
Persistent agents need separate explicit authorization. Never send passwords,
recovery codes, card details or private records through first contact or this app.
