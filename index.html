A checklist is not proof that service is active.

Residential

Use the $89 remote PC offer only for a home user's own PC: one main issue, up to 60 minutes. Work for an employer, organization or business account needs a separate business review—even from a home office.

Confirm fit, consent, commercial tool rights, appointment, written scope and total. Home visits and other device help are by quote.

Business

No monthly price or active managed coverage is established here. Confirm devices, users, duties, actual capacity, service hours, licensed tools, support allowance, escalation and price before a pilot.

Release gates

Verify the contracting identity and terms with qualified advisers. Test access consent/revocation, ticket routing, billing and any agreed recovery process on authorized test data. Contact delivery and production deployment remain unverified here.

No guaranteed response, fix, recovery or security outcome. No round-the-clock support or compliance certification is claimed. Do not use employer assets, accounts, licenses, customer data or time.
